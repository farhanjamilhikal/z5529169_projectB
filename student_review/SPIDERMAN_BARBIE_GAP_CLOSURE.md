# Spider-Man versus Barbie gap-closure decision

## Locked scope

The official FINS3645 product remains the ten equity, cryptocurrency and
combined systematic funds. The Spider-Man: No Way Home versus Barbie extension
is a separately labelled external-data research lab because SONY, MAT and WBD
are not members of the supplied 50-equity universe. It cannot be represented as
an official course-data fund or as evidence that film marketing caused returns.

The lab replaces the earlier unsupported franchise tree with:

- six predeclared campaign or release dates;
- verified company-exposure channels;
- public adjusted-price event windows for SONY, MAT and SPY;
- post-release IMDb aggregate ratings which are excluded from trading;
- source-strength and limitation fields;
- an app explanation separating movie assurance, finance classification and
  portfolio performance.

## Predeclared decision rule

The finance-augmented lexicon may be retained for research only when both gates
pass:

1. On a general or movie-review holdout, accuracy and macro-F1 must each
   deteriorate by no more than 0.01, and no class recall may deteriorate by more
   than 0.05.
2. On a licensed expert-labelled finance holdout, macro-F1 must improve by at
   least 0.02, and no class recall may deteriorate by more than 0.05.

The current NLTK movie result passes the first accuracy and macro-F1 gates. The
second gate has not been tested. Therefore, the correct statement is that the
lexicon is a transparent research extension which changes headline scores and
produces a small historical portfolio difference. It is not commercially
validated.

## Film-event result boundary

The event table reports company and SPY returns around three events for each
film. Six events are too few for a general return-prediction claim, and the
windows do not isolate earnings, macroeconomic news or other company events.
The correct language is **association around predeclared film events**, never
marketing causation or investable alpha.
